Why this matters more than the printed odds

The overall odds on a ticket never change — they are fixed the day the game goes to print. What changes every day is how much of the prize pool is still there. Two tickets with identical printed odds can be worth very different amounts depending on whether the jackpots have already been claimed, and a state lottery is under no obligation to stop selling a game once they have.

Colorado prizes-remaining questions

Which Colorado scratch-off has the most top prizes left?

$50 Frenzy currently has the most top prizes unclaimed of any Colorado scratch-off — 1275 of its original 4224. Across every Colorado game we track there are 3,898 top prizes still unclaimed.

Which Colorado scratch-offs have no top prizes left?

12 Colorado games have had every top prize claimed and are still on sale: $3,000,000 Millionaire Maker, $500,000 Crossword, MONOPOLY™, Wild Cherry Crossword, LADY LUCK, Denver Nuggets, MERRY AND BRIGHT, Crossword Multiplier, and others. A state lottery is not required to pull a game once its jackpots are gone, which is exactly why it is worth checking before you buy.

Does more prizes remaining mean a better ticket?

Not on its own. A brand-new game has every prize left and has also sold almost no tickets, so the share still unclaimed matters more than the raw count — which is why this table shows both. What actually moves the value is how many prizes remain relative to how many tickets are left in circulation.

How current are these Colorado prize counts?

They are re-read every morning from the Colorado Lottery's own published prize tables, so they reflect the most recent figures the state has released. Lotteries typically update once a day, so a prize claimed this afternoon may not appear until tomorrow.
