Best overall odds is NOT the best game — and we measured it

Nearly every scratch-off site ranks games on this one number. We tested it. Over 90 days of daily prize counts we compared eight ranking rules, and ranking by best overall odds came out worst: the highest collapse rate of any rule at 1.86%, against 1.47% for simply ranking on expected value, and middling returns besides.

The reason is mechanical rather than statistical. Overall odds measure how often a ticket wins any prize — very often a prize equal to the ticket price. A game at 1 in 2.29 is a game handing back a great many small wins. It is not a measure of what the game is worth, and it never changes: the odds are fixed the day the game is printed, while the prizes still unclaimed change every single day.

Florida best-odds questions

Which Florida scratch-off has the best odds?

$15MM DIAMOND SPECTACULAR ($30) has the best overall odds of any Florida scratch-off on sale, at 1 in 2.61. Overall odds describe how often a ticket wins ANY prize, including a prize equal to the ticket price, so the game with the best odds is usually the one handing back the most small wins rather than the one worth the most.

Do the best overall odds mean the best scratch-off to buy?

No. We backtested eight ranking rules over 90 days of daily prize counts. Ranking by best overall odds produced the highest collapse rate of any rule we tested — 1.86%, worse than simply ranking by expected value at 1.47% — with middling returns. Overall odds measure how OFTEN a ticket wins something, not what the game is worth. A game at 1 in 2.29 is a game handing back a lot of small wins.

What are the worst odds on a Florida scratch-off?

BONUS BLOWOUT has the longest overall odds we currently list for Florida, at 1 in 9.78. Longer odds are not automatically worse: a game that wins less often can still carry more value per ticket if the prizes are larger and enough of them are unclaimed.

What should I look at instead of overall odds?

How much of the prize pool is still unclaimed. Overall odds are fixed the day a game is printed and never change; the prizes remaining change every day as people claim them. Two tickets with identical printed odds can be worth very different amounts depending on how many top prizes are left, which is what this site recounts every morning.
