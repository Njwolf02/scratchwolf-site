An education tool — not a gambling service

ScratchWolf does not sell lottery tickets, accept wagers, or award prizes. We analyze publicly available data published by state lotteries to help players make informed decisions. Every lottery game has a negative expected return long-term — we show you the math, and we'll always tell you when the smartest play is not to play.

ScratchWolf is not affiliated with or endorsed by any state lottery. Lottery data is collected from official public sources and may be delayed. Results are never guaranteed.
