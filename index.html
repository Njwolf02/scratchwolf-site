What the state lottery publishes, made useful

01

Prize counts, refreshed daily

State lotteries disclose how many top prizes remain on each game. We collect those numbers every morning, so you can skip a ticket whose jackpots were all claimed months ago.

02

One score per game

Odds, prize pool, expected value, and trend, combined into a single 0–100 score. A $5 game in Kentucky is compared against other Kentucky games, because that's where you're actually standing in line.

03

The "skip these" list, free

Games with dead prize pools get flagged for everyone, no subscription required. Knowing what not to buy is half the point.

04

Alerts when things change

A top prize gets claimed, a game starts selling out, a fresh game launches with a full pool. You'll hear about it the same morning we do.

A data tool, not a gambling service

ScratchWolf doesn't sell tickets, take wagers, or award prizes. We analyze the public data that state lotteries are required to publish. Every lottery game loses money on average — we show you that math too, and when the smartest play is not to play, the app says so.

ScratchWolf is not affiliated with or endorsed by any state lottery. Data is collected from official public sources and may be delayed; results are never guaranteed. For adults 18+ (19/21 where required). If gambling is causing you harm, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
