Which Kentucky scratch-off has the best overall odds?

Kentucky Jackpot has the shortest published overall odds in Kentucky, at 1 in 2.45. Worth knowing: overall odds tell you how OFTEN a ticket wins something — usually a small prize, often just the ticket price back — not how much the game is worth. We backtested eight common strategies and picking on overall odds came out the weakest of them, so treat it as one number among several rather than the answer.
