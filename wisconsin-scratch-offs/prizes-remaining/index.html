Why this matters more than the printed odds

The overall odds on a ticket never change — they are fixed the day the game goes to print. What changes every day is how much of the prize pool is still there. Two tickets with identical printed odds can be worth very different amounts depending on whether the jackpots have already been claimed, and a state lottery is under no obligation to stop selling a game once they have.

Wisconsin prizes-remaining questions

Which Wisconsin scratch-off has the most top prizes left?

$50S & $100S EXPLOSION currently has the most top prizes unclaimed of any Wisconsin scratch-off — 50134 of its original 72468. Across every Wisconsin game we track there are 59,026 top prizes still unclaimed.

Which Wisconsin scratch-offs have no top prizes left?

25 Wisconsin games have had every top prize claimed and are still on sale: $1,000,000 CROSSWORD, COLORFUL CROSSWORD, EXCEPTIONAL CROSSWORD, 200X THE CASH, CROSSWORD MULTIPLIER, 100X THE CASH, EXTREME GREEN, LUCKY WORD CROSSWORD, and others. A state lottery is not required to pull a game once its jackpots are gone, which is exactly why it is worth checking before you buy.

Does more prizes remaining mean a better ticket?

Not on its own. A brand-new game has every prize left and has also sold almost no tickets, so the share still unclaimed matters more than the raw count — which is why this table shows both. What actually moves the value is how many prizes remain relative to how many tickets are left in circulation.

How current are these Wisconsin prize counts?

They are re-read every morning from the Wisconsin Lottery's own published prize tables, so they reflect the most recent figures the state has released. Lotteries typically update once a day, so a prize claimed this afternoon may not appear until tomorrow.
