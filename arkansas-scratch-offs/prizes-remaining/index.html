Why this matters more than the printed odds

The overall odds on a ticket never change — they are fixed the day the game goes to print. What changes every day is how much of the prize pool is still there. Two tickets with identical printed odds can be worth very different amounts depending on whether the jackpots have already been claimed, and a state lottery is under no obligation to stop selling a game once they have.

Arkansas prizes-remaining questions

Which Arkansas scratch-off has the most top prizes left?

$50 or $100! 2026 Ed currently has the most top prizes unclaimed of any Arkansas scratch-off — 22518 of its original 78608. Across every Arkansas game we track there are 32,867 top prizes still unclaimed.

Which Arkansas scratch-offs have no top prizes left?

4 Arkansas games have had every top prize claimed and are still on sale: $200,000 Platinum Jackpot, Mega Cash Crossword, 50X the Cash, X50 High Roller. A state lottery is not required to pull a game once its jackpots are gone, which is exactly why it is worth checking before you buy.

Does more prizes remaining mean a better ticket?

Not on its own. A brand-new game has every prize left and has also sold almost no tickets, so the share still unclaimed matters more than the raw count — which is why this table shows both. What actually moves the value is how many prizes remain relative to how many tickets are left in circulation.

How current are these Arkansas prize counts?

They are re-read every morning from the Arkansas Lottery's own published prize tables, so they reflect the most recent figures the state has released. Lotteries typically update once a day, so a prize claimed this afternoon may not appear until tomorrow.
