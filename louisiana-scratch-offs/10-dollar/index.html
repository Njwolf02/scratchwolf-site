A shorter-odds ticket is not automatically the better buy

Overall odds measure how often a ticket wins any prize — very often a prize equal to the $10 you paid for it. We compared eight ways of ranking games over 90 days of daily prize counts, and ranking by best overall odds came out worst: the highest collapse rate of any rule at 1.86%. What moves the value is how much of the prize pool is still there, which changes every day while the printed odds never do.

$10 Louisiana scratch-off questions

Which $10 scratch-off has the best odds in Louisiana?

Louisiana Seasons has the shortest overall odds of any $10 Louisiana scratch-off on sale, at 1 in 3.24. Overall odds count ANY prize, including one equal to the $10 you paid, so this is the game that wins most often rather than the one worth the most.

What is the biggest prize on a $10 scratch-off in Louisiana?

$250,000 on Hit It Big, with 3 still unclaimed. A larger printed top prize does not make a ticket better value on its own — what matters is how many of those prizes are actually left.

Are $10 scratch-offs better than cheaper tickets?

Usually the odds are shorter and the payout percentage higher as the price rises, and that is a real effect rather than folklore. It is not the strongest signal though: how much of the prize pool is still unclaimed matters more than the price on the front of the ticket.

How many $10 scratch-offs are on sale in Louisiana?

4 right now, and 3 of them still have at least one top prize unclaimed. We recount these figures every morning from the Louisiana Lottery's own published prize tables.
