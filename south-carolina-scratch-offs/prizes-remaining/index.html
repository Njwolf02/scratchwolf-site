Why this matters more than the printed odds

The overall odds on a ticket never change — they are fixed the day the game goes to print. What changes every day is how much of the prize pool is still there. Two tickets with identical printed odds can be worth very different amounts depending on whether the jackpots have already been claimed, and a state lottery is under no obligation to stop selling a game once they have.

South Carolina prizes-remaining questions

Which South Carolina scratch-off has the most top prizes left?

Hit $500 currently has the most top prizes unclaimed of any South Carolina scratch-off — 24900 of its original 70213. Across every South Carolina game we track there are 62,291 top prizes still unclaimed.

Which South Carolina scratch-offs have no top prizes left?

15 South Carolina games have had every top prize claimed and are still on sale: Millionaire Bonus, Giant Jumbo Bucks, $200,000 Extra Play, 50X, My Money Maker ($5), Sapphire Surprise, Lady Jumbo Bucks Crossword, Bingo, and others. A state lottery is not required to pull a game once its jackpots are gone, which is exactly why it is worth checking before you buy.

Does more prizes remaining mean a better ticket?

Not on its own. A brand-new game has every prize left and has also sold almost no tickets, so the share still unclaimed matters more than the raw count — which is why this table shows both. What actually moves the value is how many prizes remain relative to how many tickets are left in circulation.

How current are these South Carolina prize counts?

They are re-read every morning from the South Carolina Lottery's own published prize tables, so they reflect the most recent figures the state has released. Lotteries typically update once a day, so a prize claimed this afternoon may not appear until tomorrow.
