Why prizes remaining matters more than the printed odds

The odds on a ticket are fixed the day the game is printed. What changes every day is how much of the prize pool is still there, and a lottery is under no obligation to stop selling a game once its top prizes are gone.

More remaining is not automatically better, though. A brand-new game has every prize left and has also sold almost nothing, so the share of a game’s top prizes still unclaimed tells you more than the raw count. Each state page shows both.

Common questions

Where can I see scratch-off prizes remaining for my state?

Pick your state in the table below. Each state page lists every scratch-off on sale with how many of its top prizes are still unclaimed, re-read every morning from that state lottery’s own published prize tables. We track 38 states.

Which state sells the most scratch-offs with no top prizes left?

Georgia: 39 of the 206 Georgia games we track are still on sale with every top prize claimed. Its prizes-remaining page names them.

Why do lotteries keep selling scratch-offs with no top prizes left?

A state lottery is not required to pull a game when its jackpots are claimed, and many keep selling until the remaining tickets run out. Right now 322 scratch-offs across the states we track are still listed for sale with every top prize gone — which is exactly why it is worth checking before you buy.

How often are scratch-off prize counts updated?

We re-read every state’s figures each morning. Most lotteries publish once a day, so a prize claimed this afternoon may not show until tomorrow, and a few publish less often.
